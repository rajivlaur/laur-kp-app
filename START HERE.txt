KP STUDIO  |  OFFLINE WINDOWS PREVIEW 0.1
=========================================

FREE TO USE. No account, subscription, internet connection during use,
or separate Python installation is required.

GET STARTED (64-bit Windows 10 or 11 on an Intel/AMD PC)
1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and select Extract All.
2. Open the extracted KP Studio folder.
3. Double-click Open KP Studio.cmd. Keep the app and runtime folders beside it.
   The launcher is not code-signed. If a Windows policy blocks it, do not
   disable security controls. Ask your administrator or use the source.
4. Choose New Chart. Select Birth or Horary. Enter a date (YYYY-MM-DD)
   and local 24-hour time. For example: 2008-01-01, 15:45:00.
5. Search for a place, e.g. Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. Select the matching
   location, or choose Add Custom Place and enter its coordinates and zone.
6. Select Save & Create. To make a correction later, open the chart from
   Charts and choose Edit Details, then Save & Recalculate. Save As opens
   a copy so your changes do not alter the original chart.

PLACES AND TIME
- Search 237,393 built-in GeoNames places worldwide. Try a city followed
  by a comma and a region or country to narrow the list. The atlas does not
  cover every village or street address. Custom places are reusable.
- North/east coordinates are positive; south/west are negative. Use an
  IANA time-zone ID (Asia/Kolkata) or a known fixed offset (+05:30).
  Historical clock rules can be incomplete. Use a verified fixed offset
  if needed. Fall-back clock times may require First or Second occurrence.
- Here & Now uses the clock and the selected or most recently saved
  location. It does not use an online location service.

NEW WORKSPACE TABS
- KP Overview: South Indian fixed-sign chart, ABCD significators by
  planet or house, and expandable Vimshottari periods. Click any sign
  to see all its entries and stellar lords.
- Houses, Planets & Aspects: switch between grahas/points and stellar
  cusps. Aspects use shortest longitude separation and an adjustable
  0-10 degree orb (default 3 degrees).
- Planet Significations: select any of the nine KP grahas to see Self,
  Star Lord, Sub Lord, Conjunction and Opposition sources. Dep gives
  occupied houses; Own gives cusp-sign rulership. A house filter shows
  matching planets. Aspect sources use direct occupancy/rulership, not
  recursive or node-proxy rules. No benefic/malefic grading is assumed.
- North Indian & Notes: original chart workspace and ruling planets.
- Uranus, Neptune, Pluto and Fortune are display-only, not KP
  significators. Fortune uses Ascendant + Moon - Sun without a night
  reversal. This update uses your existing chart library automatically.

CHART CONVENTIONS
- The KP NEW label in this preview uses Swiss Ephemeris mode 45,
  Krishnamurti-Senthilathiban (VP291). The exact New KP formulation in
  your reference application has not been confirmed. Compare the
  ayanamsha, ASC and a few cusps with a trusted New KP chart before use.
- The cusp tables use Placidus and derive sign lord, star lord, sub-lord,
  and sub-sub-lord from the unrounded sidereal longitude. Mean Rahu and
  opposite Ketu are used. Supported local birth years: 1801-2398.
  Some polar locations do not support Placidus; no substitute house
  system is silently used. Location entry remains worldwide.
- Horary splits star/sub intervals at zodiac sign boundaries to obtain
  249 divisions. The selected division start is the Horary ascendant;
  houses are recalculated and planets remain at the entered UTC time.
- The ruling lords and four-level significators use the conventions
  labeled in the app. Dasha dates are shown in UTC (365.25-day year).

YOUR CHARTS
- Charts, notes and custom places are stored on your PC in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\KP Studio\charts.sqlite3, not inside the ZIP.
- Charts > Back Up Library copies the local database. To restore manually,
  close KP Studio, keep a copy of the original, and replace charts.sqlite3
  in that folder with your backup (named charts.sqlite3).

VALIDATION
The KP calculations, analysis tables and offline storage workflow were
tested on Linux. The Windows launcher was not run on Windows.

If the app does not open, first make sure you extracted the whole ZIP.
For a console diagnostic, open Command Prompt in the extracted folder and
run: "Open KP Studio.cmd" --diagnostic
